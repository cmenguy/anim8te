# library/performers

One folder per performer: `library/performers/<performer_id>/base.png`, the single image every clip of that performer starts from (GDD §4 stage 1). One performer for the whole library keeps body proportions constant across clips (GDD §11, identity drift). Git-ignored except this file.

## Base image checklist (stage 1)

- [ ] Full body in frame, head to feet, with margin on all sides (aim for at least 15% above the head and below the feet so jumps and vaults stay in frame).
- [ ] Locked-off camera at roughly waist height, facing the subject, no tilt.
- [ ] Plain, static background; nothing that moves or could be read as a second person.
- [ ] One person.
- [ ] Fitted clothing: no capes, long coats, loose sleeves or long loose hair hiding limbs.
- [ ] Feet clearly separable from the floor (contrast between shoes, legs and ground).

How it was made: generated by the owner with an AI image model outside this repo (2026-10-06); model and prompt not recorded. Earlier fal FLUX candidates (generic performer in a grey studio) were rejected in favour of this one.

Checklist result:

- Full body with margin: yes, about 20% above the head and 25% below the feet; the body is about 55% of the frame height and well clear of both sides.
- Camera: straight-on, roughly waist height (judged from the deck's perspective).
- Background: concrete wall and floor, static, uncluttered, but textured with panel joints rather than plain. Accepted; the fixed edges also make camera drift easy to spot.
- One person, fitted clothing: yes.
- Feet vs floor: weak spot. Black shoes and trousers on a black deck; only the white soles stand out. Watch foot keypoints and contacts in the M0.7 to M0.9 GVHMR test; if they are poor, regenerate with lighter trousers or a lighter belt.
